Stress & Relaxation Reset

Many people are interested in meditation and mindfulness but struggle with knowing where to begin.

A relaxation experience can help people discover that they can influence their own emotional state through awareness, breathing, and focused attention.

During a relaxation reset, people can learn:

  • How to recognize tension in the body
  • How breathing affects emotional states
  • How to create a calmer mental state
  • How awareness creates the pause needed for change

Recovery & Behavior Change

As a certified SMART Recovery Facilitator, I have experience supporting individuals exploring recovery, motivation, emotional regulation, and behavior change.

SMART Recovery incorporates practical tools based on:

  • Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT)
  • Dialectical Behavioral Therapy (DBT)
  • Motivational Interviewing
  • Mindfulness practices
  • Self-management strategies

Recovery is not only about stopping a behavior. It is about understanding the patterns behind behaviors and developing healthier ways to respond.

Hypnosis & Mind-Body Change

As a Board Certified Hypnotist through the National Guild of Hypnotists, I have years of experience using hypnosis, relaxation, and mind-body techniques.

Hypnosis is not mind control. It is a natural state of focused attention and relaxation that can help people explore thoughts, beliefs, emotions, and behaviors.

My training includes hypnosis, NLP, CBT, DBT, mindfulness, and other approaches that provide a broad toolkit for personal change.
